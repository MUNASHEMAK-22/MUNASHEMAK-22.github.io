About

I came into data analysis sideways — through an Electronics & Telecommunications degree, not a business or finance one. That turned out to matter: signal noise, sensor drift, and system reliability are the same underlying problem as data quality and pipeline reliability, just wearing different clothes.

Everything on this site reflects that. My degree covered Python and the fundamentals of SQL; Power BI I taught myself from scratch, by building things that actually run — not tutorials. The ETL pipeline really processes 1,084 messy rows. The dashboards really recalculate when you filter them. If a number is on this site, I can show you where it came from.

I'm looking for roles where that combination of technical problem-solving, data, and automation can create practical business value — whether that's BI development, analytics engineering, digital transformation, or data-driven business operations.

Engineering Roots

Electronics & Telecom trained me to think in signals, thresholds, and failure modes — the same lens behind the anomaly detector in the IoT project and the validation logic in the ETL pipeline.

Self-Built Stack

Python and SQL fundamentals from my degree; Power BI added on my own — all three sharpened by shipping real pipelines and debugging them until the numbers were right.
